created for moochi's idosyncratic games class at boshi's place where we focus on an idosyncratic game dev every 2 weeks.

this grid-based word jam is inspired by Michael Brough, a game developer known for innovative game plays in often tight grid spaces, allowing the player to have creative control but with limitations (sometimes even self-destructive as in Corrypt). 

in this word jam, i want the player to have the freedom to rearrange words from existing poems, but also throw in some randomness and restrictions (especially in the ghost mode) so they must choose each step/word wisely.

the ghost mode is inspired by one of my favorite mechanics in brough's Corrypt, the mimics. i wrote this in my playlog: "i felt like i'm playing with a ghost or my mirrored self. If the first mimics level is more about guiding , the second one feels more intense that you need to hide and trap it. it follows you and if you're not careful it sticks with you, until you find ways to leave it behind and move on. One can find several metaphors here - trauma, obsession, narcissism... Iam impressed how much emotion a game like this could evoke."

it was also fun to add in a verse from a classic chinese poem about the moon. it feels easier to play with chinese than english, probably because chinese is a highly succinct and abstract language, and each chinese word can have lots of potentials.

i've never been a poetry person, but making and playing this game gave me some new perspectives on how words work, or not, and what makes a word or a series of words expressive.
